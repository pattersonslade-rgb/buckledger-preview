Buck Ledger privacy

Short version: your photos never leave your phone, and the app only goes online when you tap a button for it.

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts.
  • Photos you log, camera names, buck names, tags, bait logs, predictions, your property location and any property map you draw are stored only on your device, in your browser's storage. Photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your property location is used on the phone for sunrise, sunset, moon and shooting light. "Use my location" asks your phone once and saves the result on the phone.
  • Reading the temperature off a photo happens on the phone. The first time you tap "Read temp from photo", the reader files load from this same site; the photo is never sent.
  • The app only goes online when you tap a button for it:
    • Get weather: sends your property location (latitude and longitude) and the dates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get hourly weather. No photos, names or other data. The weather is saved on the phone.
    • Get USGS elevation (3D Terrain Sim): sends the corners of your property box to the U.S. Geological Survey's 3DEP elevation service (elevation.nationalmap.gov). The elevation is saved on the phone.
    • License key: when you enter a key, the app sends that key (with the product ID, and nothing else; for an Elite Upgrade, your Pro key is re-checked the same way) to Gumroad's license service to confirm it's a valid, non-refunded purchase. While online it re-checks the key about once a month. Gumroad handles this under its own privacy policy.
  • Those services see your internet address when you use them, like any website. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Rut, acorn, drought and crop info for your states is built into the app (no lookup is made when you pick a state). Source links open the agency's own website.
  • Purchases, support and refunds are handled through Gumroad.
  • Backups and CSV exports are files you create and keep yourself. A license isn't copied into backup files. Saved weather and elevation aren't in backups either; you can get them again with a tap.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes everything the app stored, so keep a backup.
